Hamad Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hamad Studio Discord application processes information required to provide its features.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Hamad Studio may process:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord usernames and display names.
  • Discord server, channel, role, and message IDs.
  • Server membership and role information.
  • Ticket metadata such as ticket number, creator, status, assigned staff member, and timestamps.
  • Moderation and security event information.
  • Voice channel join, leave, or move events where server logging is enabled.
  • Message edit or deletion metadata where available through Discord.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to:

  • Operate Discord ticket features.
  • Manage roles and server interactions.
  • Provide server logs and moderation records.
  • Detect destructive or abusive server activity.
  • Operate security and anti-abuse features.
  • Maintain application functionality.

3. Ticket Data

Hamad Studio may locally store limited ticket metadata, including the ticket identifier, creator ID, ticket type, status, assigned staff member, additional authorized users, and relevant timestamps.

Hamad Studio does not intentionally maintain permanent full transcripts of private ticket conversations as part of its normal ticket database.

4. Message Content

Full message content is not required for the application's normal core operation.

Certain optional moderation or logging functionality may access message content when Discord provides it and the required permissions or intents are enabled.

5. Data Sharing

Hamad Studio does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed through Discord because the application operates on the Discord platform. Discord's own Privacy Policy applies separately to Discord's processing of information.

6. Data Retention

Data stored directly by Hamad Studio is retained only for as long as reasonably required for application operation, security, moderation, or server administration.

Closed or deleted tickets may have their local operational records removed according to the application's ticket lifecycle.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and operational safeguards are used to limit unauthorized access to information processed by the application.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Requests

Users may request information about, correction of, or deletion of information stored directly by Hamad Studio where applicable.

Requests may be submitted through the official Hamad Studio Discord support or project-request system.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change.

10. Contact

Privacy-related questions may be submitted through the official Hamad Studio Discord support system.
